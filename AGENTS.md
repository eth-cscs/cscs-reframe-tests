# AGENTS.md — cscs-reframe-tests

The agent-facing knowledge for this repository lives in
[`skills/reframe-alps/SKILL.md`](skills/reframe-alps/SKILL.md): a
cheat-sheet of environment setup, Alps-specific gotchas, and the
conventions shared across the GH200 vclusters (**daint**,
**starlex**, **clariden**, **santis**) and the MI300/MI200 system
**beverin**.  It is *not* a general ReFrame tutorial.

- **AI agents** — before writing, reviewing or running ReFrame tests,
  read [`skills/reframe-alps/SKILL.md`](skills/reframe-alps/SKILL.md).
  It is the canonical cheat sheet for ReFrame on Alps; once published
  it will also be installable from
  [docs.cscs.ch/agents](https://docs.cscs.ch/agents/).
- **Human contributors** — see
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md), [`README.md`](README.md) and
  the [CSCS uenv documentation](https://docs.cscs.ch/software/uenv/)
  for contributor conventions and the user-environment workflow.
